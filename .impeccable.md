## Design Context

### Users
EV and hybrid owners, especially people with used or five-plus-year-old vehicles approaching the factory-warranty cliff. They need to understand eligibility, enter basic vehicle details, and get a clear preliminary coverage estimate without insurance jargon or a lengthy application.

### Brand Personality
Calm, plainspoken, and quietly confident. Coverage in a Click should feel like a precise consumer product that is on the driver's side: warm rather than corporate, clear rather than salesy, and energetic only at meaningful moments.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined editorial light interface built from an ice-blue canvas, white product surfaces, cool near-black ink typography, cobalt actions, and charge green reserved for successful or quote-ready states. Geist carries display hierarchy and readable UI/body copy, and Geist Mono is limited to short uppercase kickers. Motion is restrained and spatial: short fades and rises for state changes, with one slower charge moment when an estimate resolves.

### Design Principles
- Lead with lifetime certainty and radical clarity; show numbers and outcomes plainly.
- Use progressive disclosure so the quote flow feels short and understandable.
- Keep cobalt for action and charge green for meaningful positive status only.
- Preserve a precise 4px spacing rhythm, hairline borders, and restrained elevation.
- Make keyboard, focus, reduced-motion, and mobile behavior first-class.
