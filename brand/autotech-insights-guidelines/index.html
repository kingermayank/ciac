01Brand idea

A signal read through the noise.

Thermal Signal treats the brand like a sensor pointed at automotive retail. One sharp read in front, the heat of the market blurred behind it. Every asset is built from the same cut, the same green ramp and the same grid.

What it is

“AutoTech Insights is your go-to source for breaking news and bold perspectives on the investors, innovators, and founders revolutionizing the automotive landscape — no pay-to-play, just real insight into the next wave of disruptive tech and trends.”

Who reads it

The people who make and fund decisions in automotive retail.

02Logo system

One cut, five marks.

Every mark is drawn from Unbounded Bold with a single rule: each curve is replaced by a 45° facet. The same cut shapes the ati plate, the jl monogram and every frame in the system.

Micro-typography

The details that make it read as one voice
  • Two families per surface

    Inter carries the message, Spline Sans Mono labels it. Unbounded appears only inside the logo.

  • Tighten as you grow

    −1% at body, −3.5% at headings, −4.5% at display. Mono stays at +8%.

  • Numbers are heroes

    Light weight, tabular, and never smaller than the headline they support.

05Signal scan

Heat, read by a sensor.

The signature texture. A mark is read like a thermal scan: a rim of light on its edges, ambient heat around it and film grain over everything, mapped through one green ramp. Move the cursor across the stage.

Light ramp

  • Scan the marks, not photos

    The scan source is always a brand shape: ati, jl, the wordmark or data bars.

  • One scan per surface

    Pair it with flat ink or paper. Never stack two scans.

  • Grain 4–8%

    Enough to feel printed, never enough to read as noise.

  • Three planes

    Focus at 0 px. Echo at 12–26 px and 40–60% opacity. Context at 60 px or more.

  • Echo in Signal

    The echo is the focus layer recoloured to Signal and offset down-right by 6–10%.

  • Never blur type you need

    Blurred copy is always a duplicate. The readable layer stays sharp and on top.

06Pattern library

Twelve repeatable patterns.

Built from the same three rules — the 45° cut, the green ramp and the 12-column grid — so new layouts can be assembled without new drawing. P01–P06 are the core set; P07–P12 extend it for covers, print and merch.

P01

Facet-cut frame

Any image, card or highlight takes the octagon cut: 45° corners at 12–22% of the short side.

P02

Scan bands

Bars read as heat. Five lit, two pale — the data itself becomes the texture.

P03

Grid crop

The wordmark cropped by the 12-column grid, on Signal. For covers and footers only.

P04

Octagon plate tiles

ati and jl plates on a strict grid at 18% opacity, with a few lit at full strength. For backgrounds, endpapers and loaders.

P07

Chevron field

Fixed 18–24 px cell; the chevron only changes size and tone with heat. Type sits in a pocket where the field recedes.

P08

Facet shards

Strokes one wordmark-stem wide, zig-zagging on the 45° lattice; one tint at 55% opacity so joints double up. Max three chains.

P09

Construction drawing

The wordmark as a drawing: 1 px outline, a node on every vertex, every 45° facet extended as a dashed guide, metrics labelled in mono.

P10

Trace routes

16 px grid. Runs are horizontal; every lane change is a 45° jog. Faceted pads at the ends, 4 px vias along the way.

P11

Ghost numerals

Unbounded at 0.8–1.1 × frame height, white on Paper (or +5% tone on Ink), bleeding one edge. Three characters at most.

P12

Scan screen

The signal scan as a one-colour 45° diamond screen (14 px cell, diagonal = cell × √value) for print, merch and embroidery.

08Data

Numbers set large, shapes kept simple.

Charts are built from the pattern library: node rows for counts, faceted bars for shares. Direct labels, no legends, one accent for the point being made.

10Voice & tone

Plain, specific, a little contrarian.

Jon writes like an operator who has seen the demo before. Headlines make one claim, in sentence case or title case, and the deck line backs it with a fact.

V1

Say the claim

“Control Is.” Short sentences that land on a full stop.

V2

Show the number

Five of seven. Seven of seven. 13% and 45%.

V3

Name the pattern

Mirage, addiction, data war — one strong noun per story.

V4

Skip the hype

No superlatives, no “revolutionary”, no AI for its own sake.
